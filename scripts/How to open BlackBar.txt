BLACKBAR.  —  a persistent AI bar at the bottom of your Mac

INSTALL
1. Drag BlackBarAI into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Applications and double-click BlackBarAI.

FIRST LAUNCH (BlackBar isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks once)
- If you see "Apple could not verify BlackBarAI…", click Done.
- Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
  "Open Anyway" next to BlackBarAI. Confirm with your password.
- On older macOS: right-click BlackBarAI → Open → Open.

SET UP (about 1 minute)
1. The Settings window opens by itself the first time.
2. Get a free Gemini API key at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
   (or use a Claude key from https://console.anthropic.com).
3. Paste it in, click SAVE KEY, close Settings.

USE
- Click the thin black bar at the bottom of your screen, type, press Return.
- ⌃⌥Space   show / hide the bar
- ⌥B        black out the bar instantly
- ⌃⌥,       open Settings (text size, bar height, True Blackout, shortcuts)
- Esc       clear the conversation and go back to your document
- The ▭ icon in the menu bar has Settings and Quit.

Works on macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel.
