The Quiet Economics of Attention

Draft · Opening line goes here

Every app on your phone is competing for the same scarce resource, and it isn't your money. It is the few hours a day when you are awake, alert, and able to care about something. Companies have learned to measure those hours down to the second, and to price them.

What makes this market unusual is that the product and the customer are the same person. We spend our attention, and in exchange we are sold to advertisers as an audience. The cost rarely shows up on a receipt. It shows up as a book left unfinished, a conversation half-heard, a thought that never quite forms.

The answer is not to log off forever. It is to notice where our attention goes, decide on purpose where we want it to go, and build small habits, and tools, that protect it.
